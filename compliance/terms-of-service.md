## 1. What this is

CashKit is a cash-flow modelling tool. You describe your money in plain words;
a deterministic engine turns it into a forecast you can trace back to the
numbers you gave it.

The service is operated by Luca Sorgiacomo (Progress Lab), Italy. By using it
you agree to these terms.

## 2. This is a beta

The service is early. It may be unavailable, it may change, and it may end.
It is offered free of charge and without any warranty. Do not use it as your
only record of anything: **keep your own copy** — Settings → Export my data
gives you one at any time, in the format we actually store.

We will give reasonable notice before shutting the service down, and long
enough to export.

## 3. It is not financial advice

CashKit does arithmetic on the figures you give it. It does not advise you.

The assistant is a command interpreter, not an adviser: it turns what you say
into an operation on your book and reports what the engine computed. It quotes
the engine's numbers; it does not invent them and it does not judge whether
your plan is a good one. Every forecast is only as complete as what you
entered — the engine cannot know about a tax, a bill or an income you have not
told it about.

Decisions about your money are yours. If you need advice, ask a professional.

## 4. Your account

One account per email address, one book per account. Sign-in is a one-time
link mailed to your address. Anyone who can read your mail can sign in as you,
so protect your mailbox.

You are responsible for what you put in your book and for having the right to
put it there.

## 5. Your data

Your book is yours. We hold it to run the service, we do not sell it, and we

You can export everything and delete everything, from inside the app, at any
time.

## 6. Fair use

There are limits, and they exist because each assistant turn costs real money:
30 turns an hour, 5 imports a day, and a daily spend cap per account. Over a
limit the assistant tells you so in a sentence and asks you to come back later
— nothing breaks and nothing is lost.

Do not attempt to work around the limits, break into other people's accounts,
or use the service to do anything unlawful. We may suspend an account that
does.

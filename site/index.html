Questions

Before you join

Do I have to connect my bank?

No. You tell CashKit what's coming — typed or spoken — or import your existing spreadsheet. It never asks for your bank login.

What if the AI misunderstands me?

You'll see it before it matters. Every change arrives as a card that spells out what CashKit understood — amount, date, how often. Apply it, edit it, or discard it.

Is this financial advice?

No. CashKit computes your own plan and answers questions about it — “can we afford it?”, “why is October low?”. It won't tell you what to buy, borrow or invest in.

Can I track what actually happened?

Yes. Record what you really spent and CashKit shows plan and actual side by side. Corrections are kept with a note, never overwritten, so your history stays honest.

Where does my data live?

On servers in the EU. You can export everything or delete your account at any time — deletion erases your data, including backups within 30 days.

When can I use it, and what does it cost?

CashKit is coming to iPhone, Android and the web, in euros. We're opening access in small groups, and the early-access list goes first. Joining the list is free; we'll tell you the price before you're ever asked to pay.

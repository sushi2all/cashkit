# ADR-0036 — The desktop app is Swiss Ledger Desk over one generated demo book

**Date** 2026-09-26 · **Status** accepted · **Type** ux / product-scope · **Source** design session 2026-09-26 (research workflow `wf_4d4c194b-4d6`: six research lenses, three style candidates, three judges, three critics)

## Context

The mobile MLP (ADR-0022) is drawn in `app.pen`. The enterprise persona of `ERP-pilot-guide.md` (controller, CFO, treasurer) needs a desktop app on the same engine: a 13-week grid, reconciliation and cutover, payment-behaviour calibration, variance and backtest, tax coverage, history and diff, gated imports. Cash-management practice and the desktop products in the category were researched first; the findings are cited in `design/desktop/REQUIREMENTS.md` §9.

Three visual directions were written and judged by three independent judges (identity, controller, front-end lead) on trust, density legibility, continuity with the mobile identity, accessibility and buildability.

## Decision

1. **Style: Swiss Ledger Desk.** The S1b "Swiss Ledger Soft Corners" tokens of the mobile app, unchanged, at desktop density: IBM Plex Sans for labels, IBM Plex Mono for every figure, date, id and stamp; monochrome ink on paper; one Signal red for danger only; Positive green only on figures that carry `+`; Warning amber only on threshold lines, Assumed badges and WHAT-IF tags; hairline dividers, 1 px ink borders, radius 4/3/2, no shadows. The judges scored it 122 against 93 (dark Trading Desk) and 86 (Calm Fintech Grid), 3 votes of 3. Full spec: `design/desktop/STYLE.md`.
2. **The receipt is the grid.** Every computed figure carries a provenance stamp (`AS OF · rN · sha · scenario · engine`); a 2 px cutover divider splits actual columns (Surface) from forecast columns (Paper); subtotals, nets and closings are engine figures only; a group the service does not total says so in the stamp voice. This is ADR-0023 at desktop scale.
3. **One demo book, generated by a script.** `design/desktop/DATASET.md` ("Delta Ingegneria 2026") is printed by `design/desktop/dataset.py` (integer minor units, Decimal factors, HALF_EVEN). Every figure on every desktop frame comes from that output. A figure that the script does not print is not drawn.
4. **`design/desktop/REQUIREMENTS.md` is the contract** for the desktop screens: 26 screens (D01–D26) behind an eight-entry rail ordered by the controller's week (Position · Close · Forecast · Receivables & Payables · Variance · Scenarios · Report & History · Book), functional requirements mapped to SDK verbs and intents, UI requirements, and §9 the twelve SDK and service requests (SR-1…SR-12) the desktop depends on. Its §8 open questions are Luca's to answer before implementation.
5. **Settled deviations from the style candidate.** Thousands grouping stays a comma (`€ 18,420.00`) as on mobile; the thin-space graft is deferred to open question 8. The book switcher sits at the top of the sidebar. Save is an Ink primary button, never red. The status bar is 28 px at the bottom. The desktop designs live in `desktop.pen`, a separate file from `app.pen`.

## Alternatives considered

- **Trading Desk (dark, terminal-grade).** Best density and contrast margins, but it contradicts ADR-0023, needs a second light token set before the first board-pack export, and its actual-column cue does not survive an uncalibrated screen. Its density rules, single-letter mnemonics and pinned cutover column were grafted.
- **Calm Fintech Grid (blue accent, cards, Inter).** The direction ADR-0023 already called vanilla; four status pairs fail AA on its grey surfaces; two typefaces for one amount; its familiarity makes users expect bank sync and editable cells. Its NO SUBTOTAL stamp, uncommitted corner mark, RECOMPUTING suffix and hover receipt were grafted.

## Risks and implications

- At 1440 px with the sidebar open, 8 of 13 forecast weeks are visible in Comfortable density; the Fit toggle and the pinned cutover column soften this, they do not remove it.
- ADR-0023 names serif display numerals and dotted leaders; the shipped S1b frames and this spec use Plex Mono and hairlines. ADR-0023 needs an amendment naming S1b as the carrier of the receipt language.
- `app.pen` flows 12–34 are still drawn in Inter with the older palette; the literal-continuity claim holds only for the four S1b frames until they are restyled.
- The desktop grid depends on service verbs that do not exist yet: weekly and daily frame payloads (D-MLP-43), group totals (D-MLP-62), a single-item read with segments (D-MLP-66/67), `at()` per period for variance rows.
- Ink-3 text on Paper passes AA by 0.04; it is used for marks and hints only.
